# Discussion numeric audit

This file lists the numerical values checked against the final pipeline outputs. Unless otherwise noted, figures are taken from the regenerated outputs in this repository.

## Key conclusions

- The full four-proxy model test RMSE is 39,225.70, versus 41,931.44 for the baseline: a 6.45% reduction. The 500-draw joint month-shuffle test gives p = 0.001996 (report as p = 0.002). This test is full-proxy versus baseline, not CCF versus macro-control.
- The macro-ratio control RMSE is 37,731.84, lower than the full-proxy RMSE.
- In the rank-reduced comparison, RMSE is 37,661.60 for the control and 37,498.37 after adding CCF. The observed point improvement is 0.43%. The paired three-month block-bootstrap median change is -0.573%, with a 95% percentile interval of -1.362% to +0.068%. Report these separately; the interval includes zero.
- Rolling-origin means are 16,887.25 RMSE and 0.061254 RMSElog for the full-proxy model, compared with 17,047.50 RMSE and 0.059264 RMSElog for the macro-ratio control.
- The final residual diagnostic specification is `rank_macro_plus_ccf`. Its mean growth residual is +0.019062, mean level residual is +10,800.65, Shapiro-Wilk W = 0.963778 (p = 1.39e-6), Breusch-Pagan LM = 20.9346 (p = 4.75e-6), and lag-1 correlation of monthly mean growth residuals = -0.10618.
- Across the final pipeline specifications, level R² ranges from 0.965489 to 0.978961 (report 0.965–0.979). The maximum test growth-based R² is 0.064477 (report approximately 0.0645). The value 0.981 in the earlier manuscript table belongs to a proxy-only + district-dummies diagnostic from the archived earlier analysis, not to the current pipeline metrics; do not combine it into the current range unless that specification is added to and rerun by the final pipeline.
- The +50% SDI scenario response of -0.8484% is from the original four-proxy scenario model (`full_proxy`), not from the rank-reduced CCF comparison. Identify the model when reporting it.

## Legacy contamination audit

The original within-month Spearman correlations are confirmed in the archived analysis object `results (1)(2).pkl`, table `T12b_cross_section_corr`: HED = 1.000000 and SDI = -0.975367. These are pre-reconstruction diagnostics for the obsolete price-dependent proxies. The current final pipeline does not reconstruct those obsolete indices; the values are retained as historical audit evidence and must not be described as outputs regenerated by the current pipeline.

## Recommended replacement wording for the manuscript

**Rank-reduced CCF comparison:**

> In the rank-reduced comparison, the observed test RMSE decreased from 37,662 under the macro-control specification to 37,498 after adding CCF, an absolute point-estimate reduction of 0.43%. The paired three-month block bootstrap yielded a median relative RMSE change of -0.57% (95% percentile interval: -1.36% to +0.07%), which includes zero.

**Model-fit range:**

> Across the final pipeline specifications, level R² ranged from 0.965 to 0.979, whereas growth-based R² reached a maximum of approximately 0.0645.

**SDI scenario:**

> In the original four-proxy scenario model, a 50% increase in the constructed SDI parameter changed predicted prices by -0.848%. This is a model-based response to the index construction, not evidence of a green premium or discount.

**Residual diagnostics:**

> For the rank-reduced macro-control model augmented with CCF, the mean log-growth residual (observed minus predicted) was +0.0191 and the mean level residual was +10,801. Residuals departed from normality (Shapiro-Wilk W = 0.964, p < 0.001), and a descriptive Breusch-Pagan test indicated heteroskedasticity (LM = 20.93, p < 0.001). The lag-one correlation of the 13 monthly mean log-growth residuals was -0.106. The Breusch-Pagan result should be interpreted descriptively because the test does not account for common month-level shocks or district clustering.
